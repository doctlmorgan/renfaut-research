R.I.S.E. Index™ Research Inventory

Before you begin

Please respond according to how you typically lead, not how you hope to lead or how you believe you should respond. There are no right or wrong answers.

How to complete the inventory

  • Think about your leadership across the situations you encounter most often rather than one isolated event.
  • Choose the response that best reflects what is consistently true of your current practice.
  • Trust your first reaction and avoid overthinking individual statements.
  • Select one response for every statement.
  • Allow approximately 10–15 minutes and complete the inventory in one sitting when possible.

Consent and research use

Your responses will be used to generate your individual Leadership Profile and may also be used in ongoing research examining the reliability and validity of the R.I.S.E. Index™. Demographic information is used for research and analysis and does not affect your individual results.

By selecting Start Inventory, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this information and voluntarily consent to participate.
