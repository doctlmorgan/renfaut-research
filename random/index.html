Complete the R.I.S.E. Index™ to explore your leadership strengths and growth opportunities. Your responses also support ongoing research to strengthen this equity-centered leadership inventory.

What participation involves

Complete a reflective leadership inventory based on your current practice. Your responses contribute to research examining the reliability and validity of the R.I.S.E. Index™ and will also generate your individual Leadership Profile.

81 statementsThe full adult research inventory.
One at a timeEach statement is presented individually to support a focused reflection experience.
Leadership ProfileReceive your personalized profile after submitting your responses.
English administration
The inventory is administered in English. You will be asked how comfortable you are reading and understanding professional or academic material in English. This is used for research and does not affect your Leadership Profile.
