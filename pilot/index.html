R.I.S.E. Index™ — Leadership Inventory Pilot

This developmental pilot explores how people understand and engage equity-centered leadership and organizational transformation.

Before you begin

This pilot contains 30 R.I.S.E. statements and two attention checks. There are no right or wrong answers. Respond based on how you generally think, act, or approach situations—not how you believe you should respond.

Research and instrument developmentThis pilot is being used to study and refine the R.I.S.E. Index™. Individual Leadership Profiles and archetypes are not generated from this experimental form.
  • Choose the response that best reflects what is consistently true of your current practice.
  • Complete the inventory in one sitting when possible.
  • Each statement appears on its own screen and advances after your first response.
  • You may use Back to review or correct an earlier response.
