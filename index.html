Explore Your Equity-Centered Leadership

Complete the R.I.S.E. Index™ to explore your leadership strengths and growth opportunities. Your responses also support ongoing research to strengthen this equity-centered leadership inventory.

What participation involves

Complete a reflective leadership inventory based on your current practice. Your responses contribute to research examining the reliability and validity of the R.I.S.E. Index™ and will also generate your individual Leadership Profile.

8–10 minutes Complete the inventory in one sitting when possible.
Reflective responses Respond according to how you typically lead, not how you think you should respond.
Leadership Profile Receive your personalized profile after submitting your responses.
Continue to the R.I.S.E. Index™

Detailed descriptions of the Renfaut™ principles are intentionally not presented before the inventory so that your responses reflect your own current practice rather than prior exposure to the constructs.
